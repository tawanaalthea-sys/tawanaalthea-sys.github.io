Reading Support Programme

Literacy Support for Developing Readers

This section provides guided reading support for children who need help building confidence with letters, sounds, words, sentences and comprehension.

Who Is This For?

This support is suitable for children who find reading difficult, read below their expected level, forget sounds, guess words, avoid reading aloud, or need extra practice to keep up with classroom work.

1. Letter Knowledge

Children practise recognising letters, naming letters and matching capital and small letters.

2. Sounds

Children learn vowel and consonant sounds through repeated oral practice and examples.

3. Blending

Children learn to put sounds together to read simple words instead of guessing.

4. Word Reading

Children practise reading word families, sight words and simple vocabulary.

5. Sentences

Children move from words to short sentences and build confidence reading aloud.

6. Understanding

Children answer simple questions to build comprehension and meaning.

Support Approach

How Edu-Web Helps Developing Readers

Small Steps

Children learn in short, manageable parts so they can master one skill before moving to the next.

Repeated Practice

Lessons can be replayed and repeated to help children remember sounds, words and patterns.

Confidence Building

Activities are designed to encourage children and reduce frustration around reading.

Adult Guidance

Parents and teachers receive clear notes to guide practice and support children effectively.

Parent and Teacher Note

Children who struggle with reading often need patience, repetition, encouragement and structured practice. Use these lessons in short sessions, repeat difficult parts, and praise effort as well as correct answers.
